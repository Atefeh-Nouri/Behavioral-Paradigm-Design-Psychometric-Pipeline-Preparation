**Selected Paradigm**: The Dual-Delay AX-CPT Protocol.

## 2. Experimental Design Specification
- Total Trials: 400 experimental trials.
- Factorial Structure: 2 Delays × 4 Trial Types.
- Global Distribution: AX 70%, AY 10%, BX 10%, BY 10%.
- Block Structure: 4 blocks of 100 trials with ABBA/BAAB counterbalancing.
- Randomization Constraints: Max 4 consecutive AX, max 2 consecutive non-targets.
- Calibration & Practice Protocol: Motor calibration + criterion practice (≥80% accuracy).
